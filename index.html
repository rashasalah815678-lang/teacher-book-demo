Chapter 02 · Build understanding

Understanding the Foundations

Al-Qira'iyyah is a foundation for correct Quran recitation.

Key learning points

  1. Meaning of Al-Qira'iyyah: enabling the learner to read what they see fluently. Understanding the meaning is not the main objective at this stage.
  2. Build the basics: focus on letters, short vowels, Shaddah, Tanween, and Sukoon.
  3. Do not rush: weak foundations can affect later Tajweed and accurate recitation.
  4. Observe the learner: identify what the student can read independently and what needs guided practice.
Remember
Strong foundations are not a delay in learning. They make later learning stable.
